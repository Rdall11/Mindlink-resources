Commercial structure
Why there's no performance kicker

You've seen performance-based pricing before. The previous contractor's Phase 4 was contingent on hitting a floor. Here's why Mindlink doesn't work that way on a build.

Performance-based pricing on a build transfers risk in the wrong direction. Before a single campaign runs, we don't know your conversion rate, your offer strength, your closing ability on calls, or which ICP segments actually have liquidity right now. Taking a result stake on variables we don't yet control isn't partnership. It's exposure for both sides.

The right structure: you pay for a Discovery that tells you exactly what to build. You pay for a build you own outright. Once the system is live and producing real data, a retainer or revenue share conversation becomes rational. Not before. That's the sequence that protects you.
