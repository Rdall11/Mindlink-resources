03 / Delivery Standard

What I hold this build to.

Every build decision is measured against three questions. This is the standard I operate to regardless of delivery pressure or timeline.

Must Ship
  • One complete golden journey end to end
  • Client-owned infrastructure and accounts throughout
  • Phase gates respected, no premature builds
  • Exact-version approval before any investor publication
  • Clean handover documentation at every phase
Must Measure
  • Time saved versus manual baseline
  • Extraction accuracy on real deal documents
  • Design-partner confidence and renewal signal
  • Phase completion against agreed gates
  • Reviewer confidence at each approval stage
Must Refuse
  • AI calculating or approving financial outcomes
  • Any publication without explicit human approval
  • Contractor-owned accounts or platform access
  • Scope creep that erodes the trust architecture
  • Premature feature builds before foundation is proven
