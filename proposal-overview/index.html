Where time leaks today

What being a one man show costs

  • ✓Hours on lists instead of owners. Pulling, cleaning and loading data is work a system can do overnight. Every hour you spend on it is an hour you are not talking to a seller.
  • ✓Deals that wait in a spreadsheet. When underwriting takes hours per deal, you look at fewer deals, and good ones go to the buyer who answered first.
  • ✓Small jobs that pull you back in. A leak, a contractor quote, a filing deadline. Each one is small. Together they keep you from being hands off.
  • ✓No record of what anything costs. Right now nobody tracks how many hours each process takes. So you cannot see where the money and time actually go.
We measure before we build. As we discussed, discovery records how many hours each process takes you today. Once the systems are live, the dashboard shows the hours saved against that baseline. You see the progress in numbers, not in promises.

01How it works
Do I have to replace DoorLoop or Mojo?
No. We build around the tools you already use. If one of them is holding you back, we tell you why and you decide.
Why start with discovery instead of building right away?
Because the build needs a solid base. These tools make messy processes messier. Discovery gives you written SOPs and organized data first, and sets the hours baseline we measure savings against.
Will it call sellers or message tenants on its own?
Only where you say so. Flow builds lists, drafts follow ups and sends routine jobs to your approved contractors under your spend limit. Seller outreach and anything over the limit wait for your approval.
Who does the work?
I do. I lead the engagement myself and own the design and priorities from start to finish.
02Your time and data
How much of my time does this take?
Very little. The intake questionnaire, one working session in discovery, short check ins at each build milestone, and the handover walkthrough.
What access do you need, and is my data safe?
Only what we connect. Your data stays in your own accounts, and I am happy to sign an NDA first. Everything we build sits on your accounts, so you own it.
03After launch
What does it cost to run once it is built?
Only your own tool subscriptions. You pay them directly, with no markup from us. Discovery names every tool and its running cost before we build.
What happens after the two weeks of support?
The systems are yours and keep running. If you want us to maintain and improve them, we can stay on for a small monthly retainer. That is optional.
Can I learn to build it myself instead?
Yes. We also run a six week Done With You program. On our call you said you would rather have it built, so this proposal is built around that.
