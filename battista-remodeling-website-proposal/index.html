Every quote request from the site is logged with the name, date and project. Each month we compare that list with jobs you closed. The share applies only to those jobs and is due when your client pays you.
Do I have to take the care plan?
No. It is optional. The build price covers everything needed to launch, plus 30 days of support.
Who owns the website?
You do. The domain, email and Google accounts are in your name, and the site is hosted on your account.
