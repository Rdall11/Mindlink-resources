Where value leaks today

What scattered information costs a relationship business

  • ✓Time lost hunting for information. When the answer lives in five tools, people re-ask, re-enter and re-check. Small per day, large per year, and leadership updates get built by hand every time.
  • ✓Relationships that never turn into deals. Contacts sit in individual inboxes, so the firm cannot see who knows whom. Follow ups depend on one person remembering, and there is no record of what each family office invests in or who signs off.
  • ✓Warm paths you cannot see. The fastest way to a family office decision maker is a warm introduction. CM&D likely already has many. They are just not searchable.
